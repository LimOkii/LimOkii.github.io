Yifei Lu (卢逸飞)

I received my master's degree from Northeastern University under the supervision of Prof. Feiliang Ren. I am currently a Research Engineer at Qiantang Credit.

My research focuses on large language model agents, tool learning, and reinforcement learning, with a particular interest in building agents that can reason, interact with tools, and improve through experience. Previously, I held research and engineering internships at Meituan AI Search, Tencent AI Lab & Hunyuan Digital Human, and DeepLang AI.
